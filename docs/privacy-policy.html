Theatrum privacy policy

Effective: October 31, 2026. Developer: Shibi. Contact: shibicb.71@gmail.com.

Theatrum is a media player for audio and video files stored on your device.

What we collect: nothing. Theatrum has no internet permission. It does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal or device data. It contains no ads, analytics or crash-reporting services, and needs no account.

What stays on your device. To work, the app keeps these in its private storage on your phone: your playlists, favorites, play counts, play history, recent searches, video resume positions, sort and display settings, and a local error log. None of it leaves your device through Theatrum. If Android backup is on, Android may include this app data in your device backup to your Google account. That is handled by Android under Google's terms, not by Theatrum.

Permissions. Media access (audio and video) is used only to list and play your files. Notifications are for playback controls. Bluetooth connect is for choosing an audio output. Foreground service and wake lock keep playback running in the background.

Error log. Crashes are recorded only on your device. You can view the log and copy it yourself. It is never sent automatically.

Deleting your data. Clear the app's storage in Android Settings > Apps > Theatrum > Storage, or uninstall the app. Either removes everything above.

Children. Theatrum is not directed at children and collects no data from anyone.

Changes. Updates to this policy will be posted at this address.
